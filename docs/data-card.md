# Data card

Five hundred fictional accepted orders have integer fixture times, integer-cent captures, partial refunds, deliveries, cancellations and repeated event payloads. The denominator includes accepted orders at or before the maturity cutoff; cancellations remain in that population. Completion means ever observed delivered by the as-of time, even if a later event cancels the order. It is not current operational status. Events after the as-of time are excluded. Empty populations produce null rates. This is not a financial reporting standard, revenue forecast or causal business lift.

All fixtures were generated specifically for this independent project. No upstream dataset, weights, media or result numbers were imported.
